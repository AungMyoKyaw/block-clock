Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Block Clock is an offline desktop clock application.

Data Collection

Block Clock does not collect, store, track, or transmit any personal data, usage metrics, telemetry, or device identifiers.

Local Storage

Preferences (theme, 12h/24h format, always-on-top pin state, and window bounds) are stored locally on your device in settings.json inside your OS configuration directory.

Network Access

Block Clock operates offline and makes zero network requests.

Contact

For questions or support, open an issue at github.com/AungMyoKyaw/block-clock.
